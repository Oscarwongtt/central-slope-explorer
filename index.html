READ BEFORE CLASS

Teacher field notes

This is a working teaching prototype, not a validated Hong Kong route-grading service.

Two kinds of data

Real geography: street/topographic web map plus optionally live Copernicus GLO-90 elevation samples (90 m terrain grid via Open-Meteo). Suitable for broad exploration, not precise road gradient, accessibility, or safety conclusions.

Classroom dataset: three entirely simulated routes A/B/C, sharing a nominal start/end elevation and a 60 m net rise. They are not geolocated or surveyed. This ensures arithmetic and grading can be correct while real verified road profiles are not yet available.

In 50 minutes

5 min introduction · 10 min exploration · 15 min calculation · 15 min route justification · 5 min debrief. Ideally 1 device per group of 2–3 students.

Other limitations

Manual survey points are connected directly; paths are not automatically snapped to footpaths. Terrain elevations should never be treated as accurate road-surface elevations. The maximum of 100 m terrain-profile windows is only an exploratory indicator.

Mission 3's simple decision rule is artificial: it is not an official walking, wheelchair accessibility or road safety standard. Teacher should review students' written reasoning.
